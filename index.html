Dear Teacher,

YOU DESERVE THE VERY BEST!

There are so many things I have wanted to tell you, but somehow, finding the right words has never been easy.

Thank you for being one of the people who inspires me, motivates me, and makes me want to become better.

You may not always realize it, but some of the things you say, the advice you give, and even a simple sentence from you can mean so much to me.

I have always felt comfortable in your lessons, and I think that is one of the most beautiful qualities a teacher can have.

This is our final year together, and thinking about that feels a little strange. There is still so much time ahead of us, so many lessons to attend, and so many memories waiting to be made.

But I also know that one day, when we look back at these days, they will become some of the most precious memories we have.

That is why I want to appreciate every moment we have this year and remember the little things that make these days special.

Thank you for your patience, your support, your motivation, and most importantly, for simply being you.

I feel truly lucky to have known you as my teacher.

I hope that years from now, when you look back on your students, I will be one of the students you remember.

I hope you know how much your work matters and how many lives a good teacher can touch without even realizing it.

Thank you for being my teacher. Thank you for crossing my path. And thank you for making this chapter of my life so meaningful.

Happy Teacher's Day! ❤️

With love,

Rüqəyya
